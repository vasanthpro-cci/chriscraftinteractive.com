Privacy policy

ChrisCraft Interactive · Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play games published by ChrisCraft Interactive on Google Play, including ScaleUp: Cosmic Growth. ChrisCraft Interactive is the trading name of an individual developer based in India.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, phone number or contacts. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.

The game uses Google services that collect some information to work. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat it usesWhy
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactionsShowing ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud
Google Play Games ServicesYour Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name, avatar), achievementsSign-in and achievements
Google Play BillingPurchase and order details. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or bank details.In-app purchases such as Fusion Orbs

How we use it

  • To run the game, save your progress and unlock achievements.
  • To deliver purchases and handle refunds or support requests.
  • To show ads that help keep the game free.
  • To fix crashes and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

Ads and your choices

Ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Google → Ads. Where the law requires it, the game will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep basic records.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent you have given. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. For information held by Google, use your Google Account settings.

Security

Data sent between the game and Google services is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
